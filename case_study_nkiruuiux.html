01 / Problem & Narrative

Understanding the Challenge

EV infrastructure planning involves complex engineering data—such as electrical transformer proximity, load limits, ADA compliance, and municipal codes[cite: 1]. Commercial property developers and HOA boards needed a clean, non-technical platform that clearly maps out pre-construction site capacity before concrete is poured[cite: 1, 4].

The challenge was to design a platform where **technical site evaluations, capital planning, and turnkey project execution** are communicated seamlessly to decision-makers[cite: 1, 2, 4].

02 / Approach & Details

Designing a Structured System

To ensure property managers can evaluate site readiness effortlessly, the design breaks down complex infrastructure stages into structured component blocks[cite: 1]:

  • Design Tokens & Typography: Geometric Space Grotesk headings paired with clean Plus Jakarta Sans body copy[cite: 2, 4].
  • Structured Service Breakdown: Components displaying key benefits, audit checklists, and high-impact hardware visuals[cite: 1, 3].
  • Interactive Accordions: Clear step-by-step guidance on electrical grid assessment, permitting, and hardware installation[cite: 1, 2].
  • Persona Targeting: Tailored navigation paths for Multi-Family HOAs, Commercial Retail, and Fleet Operations[cite: 1, 2].
03 / Result & Deliverables

Design Solution & Impact

Delivered a complete, high-converting desktop platform layout and reusable Figma component UI kit[cite: 1]:

  • Figma Desktop System: Full responsive screen layouts and scalable UI library built in 2 weeks[cite: 1].
  • Clarity in Audits: Converted dense engineering data into visual cards, interactive accordions, and clear decision trees[cite: 1, 2, 3].
  • Media Curation: Curated high-resolution commercial property and EV charger media to reflect real-world installation environments[cite: 1, 3, 4].
