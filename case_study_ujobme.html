01 / Challenge & User Journey

Simplifying Complex Career Portals

Career service portals frequently suffer from cognitive overload—cluttered filter controls, unclear account handoffs between universities and candidates, and low scannability for fast-paced job applications.

The goal was to own the visual hierarchy from first sign up to daily exploration , creating distinct, intuitive paths for candidates seeking university career centers while keeping job discovery effortless.

02 / Design Approach

Visual Hierarchy & Cognitive Load Management

Applying fundamental UX psychology principles (such as Hick's Law and Gestalt principles), the interface prioritizes high-value actions while eliminating visual noise:

  • Split-Screen Entry Architecture: Direct distinction between direct UJobMe user login and university-partnered Career Center access.
  • Frictionless Filtering & Choice Architecture: Unified search bar grouping keyword search, contract type, and geographical location into a single pill-shaped component to minimize cognitive effort.
  • Scannable Opportunity Cards: Structured job listings highlighting role title, location, contract type, and fresh post timestamps in a clean layout with generous whitespace.
03 / Implementation & Scannability

Company Insights & Dynamic Layouts

To give applicants confidence before applying, employer profile pages balance corporate metrics with authentic media:

  • Structured Metadata Banner: Highlighting company size, sector, and location using crisp icon-label groupings.
  • Culture Media Carousel: Integrated visual gallery giving applicants immediate insight into workplace environment.
  • Responsive Grid Layouts: Engineered layout components that adapt fluidly across desktop, tablet, and mobile views.
