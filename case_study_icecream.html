01 / Discovery & Feed

High-Impact Visual Appetite Appeal

Food delivery apps often suffer from generic text-heavy lists that fail to invoke culinary desire. For artisanal gelato, texture, toppings, and scoop vibrance are primary purchase motivators.

**Icey** solves this through card layouts featuring close-up photography, delivery time badges, toppings availability tags, and an instant category bar (`Double Oreo`, `Lotus Biscoff`, `Pistachio`, `Cookies & Cream`).

02 / Customization

Granular Flavor & Topping Selection

Allowing customers to personalize their dessert options without cluttering the screen:

  • Popular Flavors List: Radio selection for Chunky Crushed Oreo, Mint Cookies & Cream, and Chocolate Oreo Fudge.
  • Clear Pricing Indicators: Direct itemized pricing per customization option.
03 / Portioning & Cart

Tactile Sizing & Instant Cart Hand-Off

  • Visual Tub Portioning: Circular thumbnail selectors for Small, Medium, and Large portions.
  • High-Contrast Action CTA: Full-width Raspberry Rose (`#D81B60`) `Add to cart` button aligned directly within bottom thumb reach.
