01 / Problem & Narrative

Communicating Scent Digitally

Online perfume shopping faces a fundamental barrier: buyers cannot smell the product before purchase. Traditional platforms rely on generic descriptions that fail to convey maceration quality, projection strength, or sillage longevity.

The challenge was to design a digital storefront where **evocative brand storytelling, ingredient transparency, and visual richness** recreate the sensory prestige of luxury perfume boutiques.

02 / Approach & Details

Interactive Scent Pyramids

To give buyers complete clarity on scent behavior across dry-down phases, the product page breaks down the note pyramid into clean, color-coded pills:

  • Top Notes: Highlighting initial opening accords (e.g., Bergamot, Pink Pepper).
  • Heart Notes: Mapping the core floral/spicy identity during wear (e.g., Black Rose, Sambac Jasmine).
  • Base Notes: Showcasing deep lingering sillage ingredients (e.g., Aged Oud, Golden Amber, Soft Musk).
  • Sizing & Cart Integration: One-tap volume selections (50ml / 100ml) with direct feedback animations.
03 / Result & Taxonomy

Structured Fragrance Taxonomy

Organized fragrance collections into mood-based and family-driven grid layouts to streamline discovery for both connoisseurs and casual buyers:

  • Olfactory Categories: Modular cards for Floral, Woody Oud, Fresh Citrus, and Nocturnal Night scents.
  • Frictionless Experience: Clean light-mode contrast card containers paired with dark hero section transitions.
