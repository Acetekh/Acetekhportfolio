01 / Splash & Brand Hook

First Impression & Immediate Hook

Fashion mobile apps frequently drop users directly into busy product catalogs without setting an aspirational brand tone, leading to high initial drop-off rates.

The initial **AWear Splash Screen** uses a full bleed editorial imagery approach combined with a clean glass-pill brand badge (`AWear Dress with pleasure`) to establish an exclusive editorial aesthetic immediately upon launch.

02 / Progressive Journey

Guiding Discovery Frame by Frame

The onboarding sequence breaks down key app value propositions into digestible visual frames:

  • Discover Trending Styles: Introducing seasonal wardrobe curation and trend intelligence.
  • Timeless Looks & Edge: Highlighting capsule wardrobe building for versatile everyday wear.
03 / Ergonomics & Wishlist

Thumb-Zone Accessibility & Hand-Off

  • Frosted Glass Bottom Sheets: Placing copy and progress controls within a subtle backdrop blur overlay ensures WCAG AAA text legibility regardless of underlying photo brightness.
  • Build Your Wishlist (Screen 5): Concluding the flow by inviting users to curate favorites and save lookbooks before entering the main feed.
